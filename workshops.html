Workshops I've Delivered

Exploring the Hidden World of Soil, Plants & Insects

This workshop was delivered at Oakhill Food Justice Farm and was organised in collaboration with other members of the community who had access to larger and more powerful microscopes.

The workshop introduced participants to different types of microscopes and explored how different microscopes can be used for different purposes. My microscope, while less powerful, has a large video screen, allowing learners to collect insects and leaves and examine them together in an interactive way.

Using the more powerful microscope, we were able to examine and compare different soils and explore what compost looks like up close. This provided an opportunity to connect hands-on scientific observation with the natural environment around the community garden.

Audience: Community learners Format: Hands-on / Demonstration Location: Oakhill Food Justice Farm Focus: Microscopy / Soil / Plants / Insects

Student Newspaper Club

While working for Navitas Darwin at Charles Darwin University, I wanted to create a project that students could work on outside of class. I recognised that students could benefit from having a social and creative activity to participate in during their free time, particularly in a city where there were limited activities available to them.

I established a student newspaper club and ran weekly meetings where students brainstormed the name of the newspaper, discussed roles and responsibilities, and developed ideas for articles. Students were able to contribute according to their interests and strengths.

As the project developed, I deliberately stepped back and gave the students increasing ownership of the newspaper. By the end, I was largely hands-off, with only the Chief Editor reviewing grammar, spelling and vocabulary. The group successfully produced monthly issues independently.

Audience: Adult students Format: Weekly group project Duration: Ongoing Skills: Collaboration / Writing / Leadership / Creativity

The Football Review — From Fan to Club Photographer

While playing Aussie Rules for Darebin Football Club, I thought it would be funny to write a review of a match from the perspective of someone who didn't know the rules of Australian football — something that, despite playing, I still don't completely understand.

The review was a hit with the club, and I was asked to write one every week. I then expanded the idea by offering to interview players and coaches and create a Photoshop concept image to accompany each review.

The project eventually developed into an official role with the club, when I was asked to become the Photoshop content creator for the Darebin Women's VFL team.

Audience: Football community Format: Writing / Interviews / Digital Media Tools: Adobe Photoshop Outcome: Official VFL Women's team content role
